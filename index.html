This Privacy Policy explains how PromptSher (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) handles information when you use the mobile application PromptSher – AI Prompt Generator.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall and do not use the App.

1. Summary

PromptSher is a gallery app for AI photo prompts. Most data stays on your device. We do not require you to create an account. We do not sell your personal information.

The App needs internet access to load the prompt catalog and images from our content servers. It may open third-party AI services (such as ChatGPT or Gemini) only when you choose to. Optional local reminder notifications require your permission.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored only on your device

The App may store the following locally on your phone (for example using on-device storage):

  • Cached prompt catalog (IDs, image URLs, prompt text, categories, keywords) for faster loading and offline use
  • Cached image data / image URL records to help display photos you already viewed
  • Favorites (photo IDs you mark with a heart)
  • Collections (names and photo IDs you save)
  • Search and browse preferences related to how the App works on your device

This local data is not uploaded to us as a user profile. The current version of PromptSher does not create an online account for you.

2.2 Information processed when you use online features

When the App is online, it may request:

  • Prompt catalog data from our content host, currently: https://image.photoprompts.fyi/prompts.json
  • Images from the image URLs listed in that catalog (hosted on our content domain / CDN)

Technical details that are normal for any internet connection may be processed by hosting and network providers (for example IP address, device/network type, time of request). That is needed to deliver files. It is not used by PromptSher as a personal account profile.

2.3 Information we do not intentionally collect

In the current version of PromptSher, we do not intentionally collect or require:

  • Name, email, or phone number to use the core App
  • Payment card data inside the App (purchases through Google Play, if any, are handled by Google)
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, microphone, or camera access
  • Social login (Google / Facebook login inside the App)

3. How we use information

We use information only to:

  1. Show prompts and photos from the remote catalog
  2. Cache content on your device for speed and so photos do not vanish when you refresh
  3. Update the local catalog when new photos, prompts, categories, or keywords are published
  4. Save favorites and collections on your device
  5. Let you search prompts using keywords and optional category filters
  6. Send optional local reminder notifications if you allow notifications
  7. Open third-party AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, or their websites) when you tap those buttons
  8. Copy prompts to your clipboard when you choose Copy Prompt

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule local reminders on your device (for example a daily message encouraging you to try new prompts).

  • You can turn off notifications in your phone’s system settings at any time.
  • These reminders are local device notifications. They are not exact alarm-clock or calendar features.
  • The App does not use the restricted USE_EXACT_ALARM permission.

5. Clipboard

If you use Copy Prompt, or tap ChatGPT / Gemini, the selected prompt text may be placed on your device clipboard so you can paste it. Clipboard content is controlled by your device and operating system. We do not operate a separate clipboard cloud service for this App.

6. Advertising ID

The App may declare the permission:

com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

This is the Google advertising ID permission used for ads and related measurement on Android, if and when ads are shown.

  • You can reset or opt out of ads personalization in your Google / Android settings.
  • If ads are not currently shown, this permission may still be present for future ad support.

7. Third-party services

7.1 Content hosting

Prompt lists and images may be loaded from remote servers/CDNs, including hosts under domains such as photoprompts.fyi (for example image.photoprompts.fyi). Those providers process technical request data needed to deliver files.

7.2 ChatGPT / Gemini and other AI tools

If you tap ChatGPT or Gemini:

  • The App may open the third-party app or website.
  • The App may also put the prompt on your clipboard to help you paste it.
  • Any data you submit inside ChatGPT, Gemini, or another AI service is governed by that provider’s privacy policy, not this one.

Examples (policies may change; always check the official page):

  • OpenAI / ChatGPT: https://openai.com/privacy
  • Google / Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7.3 Google Play

If you download the App from Google Play, Google may collect install, crash, and usage data under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

7.4 Advertising partners (if ads are enabled)

If ads are enabled, advertising partners such as Google AdMob may process device advertising identifiers and related technical data under their own policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Data retention

  • Local cache, favorites, and collections: kept until you clear app data, uninstall the App, or we change storage in an update
  • Temporary network requests: as needed to load content; not retained by us as a user account file

Uninstalling the App typically removes local App storage, subject to your device settings.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to a mobile app (for example HTTPS for content loading where configured). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use the App on an updated device and operating system.

11. Your choices and controls

You can:

  • Deny or revoke notification permission in system settings
  • Reset or limit advertising ID / ads personalization in Android settings
  • Clear App data or uninstall to remove local cache, favorites, and collections
  • Avoid ChatGPT / Gemini buttons if you do not want to use third-party AI services
  • Use previously cached content when offline (new photos will not appear until you are online)
